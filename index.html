03 · Legal framework

Four layers of water law.

Sources: Constitution of the Republic of Azerbaijan; Water Code, Art. 2

International treaties outrank ordinary laws: if a treaty Azerbaijan has joined sets different rules on water, the treaty applies. That is why international treaties matter for domestic water law. Click a layer, or press → to open them in turn.

The Water Code declares the country’s waters, including its Caspian sector, the national wealth of the people. It defines pollution, sets up state water accounting, and in Chapter 14 deals with protection of surface and groundwater, protection zones and pollution control. Article 2 gives treaties priority.

03 · Spatial protection

Buffers around every water body.

A wider conservation zone and a stricter coastal strip.

Water body

Source: Cabinet of Ministers Decision No. 56 of 24 March 2000 (implementing Water Code, Art. 82)

The Water Code lets the Cabinet set the width and regime of protection zones. The 2000 decision creates two bands: a wider conservation zone and a narrower coastal strip with the strictest limits. Where a zone holds spawning grounds or drinking-water sources, it becomes a protected area (the leaf badge).

The Water Code does not work alone. The 1999 Environmental Protection Law sets general principles and the state ecological expertise. The Water Supply and Wastewater Law governs drinking water and discharges. Waste law matters because landfills are a main pollution pathway into rivers and groundwater.

Sources: Mondaq / BM Morrison Partners (2022); ADB (2014); Report.az (Oct 2026)

EIA is the main preventive tool for water. Before 2018, practice relied on a 1996 handbook with no legal force. The 2018 law and the 2022 rules made EIA binding, including for transboundary projects. The October 2026 amendments add numeric thresholds: less burden on small farms, but a narrower scope in agriculture. Press → to walk through the five stages.

03 · Enforcement

Three tiers of liability.

Tier 1

Administrative

Code of Administrative Offences (2015): emission and discharge limits. In 2024 the fine for littering with cigarette waste rose from 50 to 300 AZN.

Liability runs from fines to prison. Press → to add each tier. The 2024 ecocide article was a presidential initiative adopted just before COP29 in Baku and sits among crimes against peace and humanity. For water, the tools used most in practice are still administrative fines and the pollution offences of the Criminal Code.

Bilateral: Samur (Russia) · Araz (Iran) · Jandari Lake (Georgia)

Because treaties outrank national law, these conventions are part of Azerbaijan’s water law. Click a treaty to open its card, click the centre or × to close, or press → to go through them. 04 · Azerbaijan in court

Shared rivers need shared rules.

Azerbaijan now takes its water and environment claims to court, abroad and at home.

Transition to the cases. Most of Azerbaijan’s water comes from abroad and treaties prevail over domestic law, so its disputes are fought under international conventions. First the cases against Armenia, then a domestic case.

No jurisdiction over the environmental-harm claims, including water: they were not racial discrimination under CERD.

Peace agreement · Art. XV

Claims may be withdrawn

Initialled in August 2025: both states withdraw inter-state claims within a month of entry into force, which has not yet happened.

Sources: PCA; Jus Mundi; MFA of Azerbaijan; EJIL:Talk!; Armenpress and CivilNet (Nov 2026)

Azerbaijan is testing environmental treaties in court. The Bern Convention case, over damage in formerly occupied territories, includes cooperation to protect the Okhchuchay River, polluted by mining upstream. The ICJ case under the racial discrimination convention shows the limits of using a non-environmental treaty. Both may be withdrawn if the peace agreement enters into force.

Domestic case law on water is thin and rarely published. This case shows how the burden of proof works against a citizen who reports suspected pollution: inspections found no breach, so the complaints were held untrue. Press → to tell the story step by step and open the discussion question.

05 · Outlook

Four open problems.

Transboundary pollution

The main threats start upstream, beyond Azerbaijan’s jurisdiction

Scarcity and climate

Supply keeps shrinking; NDC 3.0 aims for −40% net emissions by 2035

Ageing, scattered law

A 1997 Water Code with many amendments, and no unified Environmental Code

Enforcement and participation

Rules on paper are rarely tested in court

Sources: EU Reporter (2021); UNFCCC (2025)

The framework is broad, but much of it dates from the late 1990s and is scattered across many laws. The biggest threats to water come from outside the borders, which is why treaties, monitoring data and dispute settlement matter. Watch the 2027 deadline, the climate targets, and whether the peace agreement closes the inter-state cases.

Fictional case

Azerbaijan v. Upland

The worst drought in 50 years. Upland’s harvest is failing.

Upland fills its Tala reservoir to water its fields. For four months, the river’s flow into Azerbaijan falls by 30%.

Downstream farms lose part of their harvest; two villages need water trucks.

The dam and its rules were notified in 2019, for normal years. Both states are in the UNECE Water Convention, with no water-sharing deal.

Fictional facts · rules from the UNECE Water Convention (1992) and the UN Watercourses Convention (UNWC, 1997)

You are the judge. Read the facts aloud, then explain the roles: about half the room are jurors, and the rest are counsel for Azerbaijan (claimant) or for Upland (respondent). Phones assign the role when the code is scanned and show each counsel a brief for the question on screen; the counsel who opens each question is named on their phone. Each question has two steps. In the hearing, counsel speak for about a minute each, and you may question them. Then the jury votes on phones (add raised hands with + and −, or keys 1–3, for jurors without a phone). After the third question the screen shows what the jury decided, and the last screen is your ruling: it starts from the jury’s majority, and a click on a line (or keys 1–3) changes it, so you can depart from the jury. A note appears when two rulings pull against each other. There is no single right answer: the case pits equitable use against the duty not to cause significant harm. Press Reset only before people scan the code, because it hands out roles again.
